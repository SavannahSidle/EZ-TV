THE EZ-TV LANE

A personal familiarity layer for the television.

EZ-TV combines family-selected media, a stable resident interface, caregiver control, privacy, and flexible connectivity in one experience.

Recognition profile

People, relationships, favourite media, comforting routines, and preferred words form a private profile that shapes the screen.

Progressive simplicity

The experience can become simpler as needs change while the placement of familiar choices remains stable.

Family presence loop

A photograph, short message, familiar voice, or routine can arrive without asking the resident to learn a communication app.

Caregiver effort budget

Every feature must save more caregiver work than it creates. If maintenance becomes a hobby, the feature has failed.

Dignity boundary

No cameras, passive listening, behavioural scoring, health predictions, or covert check-ins.

Connectivity ladder

Start online. Preserve cached essentials during interruptions. Add local and USB support where reliable internet is unavailable.

One experience, many screens

The resident interface stays familiar across a computer, supported smart television, streaming device, or future hub.

Built with one real person

Wanda is the first use case. Decisions are tested against actual daily needs before EZ-TV asks other families or homes to adopt them.

CLEAR ANSWERS

What exists, what comes next, and what Wanda has to do.

Does EZ-TV work between two devices yet?

The current live demo shows the interaction on one split screen. True secure synchronization between a caregiver device and Wanda’s screen is the next development milestone.

Does Wanda need a touchscreen?

No. Touch is one optional method. The pilot will choose the easiest route for Wanda: a simple remote, caregiver control, mouse, keyboard, adaptive switch, or press-to-speak voice.

Does it require a smart television?

No. The first working pilot can run full-screen on a computer connected to a television. Smart-TV packaging and ordinary HDMI streaming devices can follow.

Does it require internet?

Remote caregiver updates require a connection. The resident player will be designed to keep essential media available during interruptions. Full USB updating and managed cellular service are later phases.

Is the EZ-TV Hub available?

No. It is a possible future packaging option for homes that need a simple HDMI device. The software must prove useful before custom hardware earns the right to exist.

Can families add Spotify, television, or computer media?

The first pilot will support private uploaded media and approved links. Streaming integrations come later because account permissions, licensing, advertisements, and changing interfaces can break the resident experience.

Is real family content used in this public demo?

No. The public prototype uses fictional names and anonymous demonstration media. Real family content will remain inside a private paired account.

Who is EZ-TV for?

The primary audience is people living with dementia and the families or care teams supporting them. The same simplified approach may also help older adults and people with cognitive, motor, visual, or technology-access barriers. Wanda is the first real pilot, with broader family and care-home use guiding the next stages.

WHY EZ-TV EXISTS

Connection should not disappear when someone enters long-term care.

EZ-TV began after designer Savannah Sidle’s mother developed early-onset Alzheimer’s disease. When her mother entered long-term care, Savannah encountered a painful gap in accessible services that helped residents remain connected with the people and experiences they knew.

The project grew from a simple purpose: help families stay present through familiar photographs, voices, music, television, and daily routines, even as memory and navigation abilities change.

Technology should carry more of the burden, not place it on the person living with dementia.

Proposed Canadian pricing for planning and demonstration only. Final features, availability, eligibility, taxes, connectivity costs, and pricing may change.
